Breakfast

Upma

Ingredients

Andhra-style Upma is made with 1 cup of semolina (rava), 2 tablespoons of oil or ghee, 1 teaspoon each of mustard seeds, cumin seeds, split urad dal, and chana dal, 1 finely chopped onion, 2 chopped green chilies, a 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, a few curry leaves, 2½ cups of water, salt to taste, and chopped coriander leaves for garnish.

Instructions

Dry roast the semolina until aromatic and set it aside. Heat oil or ghee in a pan and add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, urad dal, and chana dal. Once they splutter, add curry leaves, ginger, onions, and green chilies, and sauté until the onions become soft. Pour in water, add salt, and bring it to a boil. Gradually add the roasted semolina while stirring continuously to avoid lumps. Cook on low heat until the water is absorbed, garnish with coriander leaves, and serve hot.

Bondalu

Ingredients

Potato, Onion, Green Chilies, Curry Leaves, Gram Flour, Turmeric and Oil.

Instructions

Mash the boiled potatoes and mix them with sautéed onions, green chilies, and curry leaves. Shape the mixture into small balls. Prepare a thick batter using gram flour, turmeric, salt, and water. Dip each potato ball into the batter and deep fry in hot oil until golden brown and crispy. Serve hot with chutney or ketchup.

Rava Dosa

Ingredients

Rava Dosa is made using 1 cup of semolina (rava), ½ cup of rice flour, ¼ cup of all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds, 1 chopped green chili, a 1-inch piece of grated ginger, chopped curry leaves, salt to taste, water as required, and oil for cooking.

Instructions

Combine the semolina, rice flour, all-purpose flour, salt, and enough water to make a thin batter. Add cumin seeds, green chili, ginger, and curry leaves to the batter. Heat a griddle and drizzle a little oil. Pour the batter from a height to create a thin, lace-like dosa. Cook until the edges become crisp, drizzle a little more oil if needed, fold the dosa, and serve hot with chutney or sambar.

Punugulu

Ingredients

Punugulu is prepared using 2 cups of idli or dosa batter, 1 finely chopped onion, 2 chopped green chilies, a 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, chopped curry leaves, chopped coriander leaves, 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds, salt if required, and oil for deep frying.

Instructions

Mix the onions, green chilies, ginger, curry leaves, coriander leaves, and cumin seeds into the batter. Heat oil in a deep pan and drop small spoonfuls of batter into the hot oil. Fry them until they turn golden brown and crispy. Remove the punugulu using a slotted spoon, drain excess oil on paper towels, and serve hot with peanut or coconut chutney.

Dibba Rotti

Ingredients

Dibba Rotti is made using 2 cups of rice, ½ cup of urad dal, ½ teaspoon of fenugreek seeds, 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds, 2 chopped green chilies, a 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, salt to taste, and oil for cooking.

Instructions

Soak the rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds for about 6 hours, then grind them into a slightly coarse batter. Add salt, cumin seeds, chopped ginger, and green chilies, and mix well. Heat a thick-bottomed pan, grease it with oil, and pour a thick layer of batter into the pan. Cover and cook on low heat until the bottom becomes golden brown. Carefully flip the rotti and cook the other side until crisp. Slice and serve hot with ginger chutney or spicy pickle.

Atukula Upma (Poha Upma)

Ingredients

Atukula Upma is prepared using 2 cups of thick poha (flattened rice), 2 tablespoons of oil, 1 teaspoon each of mustard seeds and cumin seeds, 2 tablespoons of peanuts, 1 chopped onion, 2 chopped green chilies, curry leaves, ¼ teaspoon of turmeric powder, salt to taste, chopped coriander leaves, and 1 tablespoon of lemon juice.

Instructions

Rinse the poha lightly and let it soften for a few minutes. Heat oil in a pan and fry the peanuts until crisp. Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, curry leaves, onions, and green chilies, and sauté until the onions soften. Stir in turmeric powder and salt, then gently mix in the softened poha. Cook for 3–4 minutes, add lemon juice, garnish with coriander leaves, and serve warm.

Minapa Garelu (Medu Vada)

Ingredients

Minapa Garelu is made using 1 cup of whole urad dal, 2 chopped green chilies, a 1-inch piece of chopped ginger, 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds, chopped curry leaves, salt to taste, and oil for deep frying.

Instructions

Soak the urad dal for 4–6 hours and grind it into a thick, fluffy batter using very little water. Mix in the green chilies, ginger, cumin seeds, curry leaves, and salt. Heat oil in a deep pan, shape small portions of the batter into doughnut-like rings, and fry them until they become golden brown and crispy. Drain the excess oil and serve hot with coconut chutney or sambar.

Idli

Ingredients

Idli is prepared using 2 cups of parboiled rice, 1 cup of whole urad dal, ½ teaspoon of fenugreek seeds, salt to taste, and water as required.

Instructions

Soak the rice and the urad dal with fenugreek seeds separately for about 6 hours. Grind them into a smooth batter, combine them, and add salt. Allow the batter to ferment overnight. Grease the idli molds, pour in the batter, and steam for 10–12 minutes until the idlis are soft and fluffy. Let them cool slightly before removing them from the molds and serve with sambar and chutney.

Andhra Pesarattu

Ingredients

Andhra Pesarattu is prepared using 1 cup of whole green gram (moong dal), 2–3 green chilies, a 1-inch piece of ginger, 1 teaspoon of cumin seeds, salt to taste, 1 finely chopped onion, 2 tablespoons of chopped coriander leaves, and oil for cooking.

Instructions

Soak the whole green gram for 6–8 hours and drain the water. Grind it with green chilies, ginger, cumin seeds, salt, and a little water into a smooth batter. Heat a griddle and spread a ladleful of batter into a thin circle. Sprinkle chopped onions and coriander leaves on top, drizzle oil around the edges, and cook until the bottom becomes golden and crisp. Flip if desired and cook briefly on the other side before serving hot with ginger or coconut chutney.
