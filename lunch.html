Lunch / Dinner

🍗 Chicken Biryani

Ingredients

Rice, Chicken, Yogurt, Mint, Coriander, Onion and Biryani Masala.

Instructions

Marinate chicken. Cook rice partially. Layer and cook on dum until done.

🍆 Gutti Vankaya

Ingredients

Brinjals, Peanut Powder, Sesame Seeds, Tamarind, Coconut and Spices.

Instructions

Stuff the brinjals and cook slowly until soft and flavorful.
